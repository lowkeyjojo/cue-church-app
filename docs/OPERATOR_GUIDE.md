# Running a service with Cue

A guide for whoever is at the computer on Sunday. Cue has two screens: **yours** (the control window, private) and **the room's** (the TV or projector). Nothing reaches the room until you choose to show it.

## The control window

- **Left: the service plan.** Everything you'll show, in order, with section headings. Click an item to **preview** it; double-click to **show** it right away.
- **Middle: Show, Search and Library.** _Show_ has the details of the selected item, such as a page grid for slides and options for videos. _Search_ is Google Images and free photo libraries. _Library_ holds everything you've imported or saved.
- **Right: Preview and Live.** Preview is what's staged. Live is exactly what the room sees right now, including fades and blackout.
- **Bottom bar:** Blackout, Ambient, Back and Clear, the transition style, the volume and the time.

## Before Sunday: set up once

1. **Connect the TV or projector and set it to extend your desktop, not mirror it.** On Windows press **Win + P → Extend**. On a Mac open **System Settings → Displays** and make the TV an extended display. If it's mirrored, the room would see your control window.
2. Open Cue. The badge at the top right shows where the audience screen is. Click it to choose a display, switch modes, **Identify** the screens, or show the **test pattern**.
3. With the test pattern on, check that its border is visible on the TV. If the edges are cut off, raise **Settings → Output → Overscan inset** until they aren't.
4. In **Settings → Audio**, pick where video sound should go (the TV over HDMI, or the sound desk) and press **Play test tone**.
5. Give the screen the name your team uses ("TV", "Projector", "Screens") so the buttons read naturally.

## Preparing a service

- **Make a plan:** **New service plan** from the plan menu (the plan name at the top left), or **File → New Service Plan**.
- **Add things:** drag files from your computer onto the plan, drag items from the Library, or use **Add to plan** for text slides, a countdown, a black screen or a section heading.
- **Slides:** export PowerPoint or Keynote presentations as PDF and import the PDF, or make text slides in Cue. Slides with lyrics or long passages can be split into one slide per paragraph in the editor. Give a song's slides one background (a photo or a looping video) and only the words change from slide to slide; the background keeps playing.
- **Bible readings:** in a quick text slide (Ctrl/⌘ + T) choose **Scripture** and type the reference, then **Save to plan**. A long reading is spread over several slides.
- **Countdown:** **Add to plan → Ambient or countdown**. Choose a background, then a countdown _for_ a number of minutes or _until_ a time, and the message to show at zero.
- **Notes:** each plan item has private notes, such as "show when the pastor mentions Ephesus". Only you see them.
- **Taking it to church:** use **Export with media…** from the plan menu. You get a single `.cueplan` file; double-click it on the church computer and the plan opens with everything in it.

## On Sunday morning

1. Open Cue and check the badge: it should name the TV, not say "No TV connected".
2. Show the countdown or the ambient background (press **A**) while people arrive.
3. If there's a video, play a few seconds and check the sound, then press **B** (blackout) or **A** while you wait.

## During the service

| To…                                 | Do this                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Show what's in Preview              | **Space** (or click **Show on TV**)                                       |
| Go to the next or previous slide    | **→** / **←** (clickers work too)                                         |
| Show the next plan item             | It's already in Preview: press **Space**                                  |
| Show any item now                   | Double-click it                                                           |
| Black out the screen                | **B**; press it again, or **→**, to bring the picture back                |
| Show the ambient background         | **A**; any slide key brings the content back                              |
| Return to the sermon slides         | **R** (Back), at the page you left                                        |
| Make a map or photo fill the screen | **F** cycles fit, fill and blur                                           |
| Zoom into part of an image          | **+** / **−**, or scroll over the Live monitor; drag to pan; **0** resets |
| Pause or play a video               | **K**; drag the timeline to jump                                          |
| Mute                                | **M**                                                                     |

**Space means Show, even after you've clicked a button.** If you move around Cue with **Tab** instead of the mouse, Space and Enter press the button you're on, as in any app (on a library item, Enter puts it in Preview), and **Ctrl + Enter** (⌘ Enter) shows the preview.

**When the pastor mentions something you don't have:** press **Ctrl + L** (⌘ L on a Mac), type what you need and press Enter. Click an image in the results: Cue fetches the full-size picture and puts it in Preview. For pictures from YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and similar sites, Google often has no full-size picture; after a moment Cue brings in the best copy it can get instead, and the bar under the results says which one you have. Check it, then press **Ctrl + Enter** (⌘ Enter) to show it. Press **R** afterwards to go back to the slides.

Other quick ways in:

- **Paste** (Ctrl/⌘ + V) an image, or a link to one, anywhere in Cue to put it in Preview.
- **Quick text slide** (Ctrl/⌘ + T): type a verse, a phrase or an announcement and show it.
- **A Bible verse:** press Ctrl/⌘ + K and type the reference, such as `John 3:16`, `ps 23` or `1 cor 13 4-7`. Press **Enter** to put it in Preview, or **Shift + Enter** to show it straight away. A long passage comes up as several slides: press **→** to move through them. To change the wording first, choose **Open … in a quick slide**. The World English Bible (WEB) and King James Version (KJV) are built in and work without internet; pick the default in **Settings → Slides**, or switch for one passage in the quick slide.
- **Find anything** (Ctrl/⌘ + K): plan items, library media, slides and actions.
- **Free images:** in Search, switch from Google to Wikimedia Commons, Openverse or Pexels for openly licensed photos with credits.

Cue keeps a list of everything shown under **Shown today** at the bottom left, so you can bring something back with a click.

## After the service

- Show the "Thank you" ambient, or press **B**.
- Images you used from the web are kept for 30 days. Star the ones you want to keep for good, and they move into your library.

**Updates never interrupt a service.** Cue doesn't restart by itself. On Windows a new version downloads quietly and installs when you close Cue; an **Update ready** button at the top right says so, and you can leave it until you're done. On a Mac the button says **Update available** and takes you to the download.

## If something goes wrong

**The TV shows nothing.** Look at the badge at the top right. If it says no TV is connected, check the cable and that the display is set to _Extend_. Click the badge and use **Identify** to see which screen is which, or pick the display yourself. As a last resort, choose **Window** mode and drag the audience window onto the TV.

**The room sees my control window.** The displays are mirrored. Set them to _Extend_ (see "Before Sunday").

**The picture is cut off at the edges.** Raise **Settings → Output → Overscan inset** while the test pattern is showing.

**No sound from videos.** Check **Settings → Audio** (the TV or the sound desk), the volume in Cue's bottom bar, and that Cue isn't muted (**M**). Use **Play test tone** to check the route.

**Google won't load.** Without internet, the Search tab says so. Your plan, slides and library still work, because everything you've imported or shown is stored on the computer.

**"Using a lower-resolution copy".** That website didn't share its full-size image, so you have Google's small copy. Try another result if the picture looks soft on the TV.

**"From the post's preview image".** Google had no full-size picture for this result, so Cue took the picture the post or video shows when it's shared. It is the same picture, usually at a good size; the numbers next to it are its size.

**Cue closed unexpectedly.** Open it again: the audience screen comes back exactly as it was.

**Something else.** **Help → Show Log Files** opens Cue's log, which helps whoever looks after the computer.

## Rehearsal checklist

- [ ] The display is extended, and the badge names the TV
- [ ] The test pattern's border is fully visible
- [ ] Video sound comes out where it should (test tone)
- [ ] The plan runs top to bottom with **Space** and **→**
- [ ] Search works on the church's internet connection
- [ ] **B**, **A** and **R** behave as expected
